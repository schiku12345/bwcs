Admin Panel
Control everything live — teams, scores, timer, screens, maps. Open on your phone or second monitor.
Quick Start:
  1. Open Admin Panel on your control device
  2. In OBS: Add Source → Browser → paste Overlay URL → set 1920×1080
  3. Add your teams & players in Teams
  4. Select your starting screen and click STARTING in the Overlay Screen section
  5. When ready, switch to GAME and hit Start Timer
